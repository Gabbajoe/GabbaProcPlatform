Method & data quality

What is measured, what is shared and where the limits are.

What the numbers mean

The addon observes supported effects triggered by your own character. Rates measure procs per eligible combat minute, not probability per hit. Attack speed, targets, talents and equipment affect the result.

Data checks

Uploads are checked for structure, counter consistency, ownership and duplicates. Extreme rates are withheld from public statistics. Plausible fabricated data cannot be reliably detected; these are community observations, not independently verified logs.

History & gaps

The server can preserve only data that reaches it. WoW writes SavedVariables on reload or logout. Older addon versions retain only a rolling history; missing fights cannot be reconstructed from cumulative counters.

What is uploaded

Supported effect IDs, area groups, counters, combat time, retained segments and stable character/collection identifiers. Name and realm are optional. Account folder names, installation paths, chat and entire SavedVariables files are not uploaded.

Names & deletion

Without names means pseudonymous: stable identifiers still connect contributions. Removing a public name cannot remove copies already downloaded by others. Device access can hide names or delete its contributions. Backups may retain deleted data until their retention period expires.
