Privacy notice

Controller and scope

Nils Keller · Mittlere Hasengasse 6 · 64625 Bensheim · Germany

Privacy contact: gabbaproc@kellerkind.info. This notice covers the GabbaProc website and companion data sent to its server. Updated: 20 October 2026.

Website, hosting and technical logs

The website, database and upload processing run on a self-managed server on Hetzner’s German network (Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestraße 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany). Technical logs process IP address, access time, requested URL, HTTP status, transfer size, browser identification and, where supplied, the referring page. The hosting provider supplies the infrastructure.

The purposes are serving the website, troubleshooting and preventing abuse. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in a secure, functioning service. Domain logs rotate daily, retaining seven rotated files in addition to the current file. General Apache logs retain fourteen daily rotations. Relevant extracts may be preserved until a specific security incident is resolved.

Language and local storage

The website stores your explicitly selected language as “language” in your browser’s local storage. Otherwise it uses the browser language, falling back to English. The setting remains until changed or the site data is cleared. This storage serves only your requested language preference (Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG; Article 6(1)(f) GDPR where personal data is involved).

We currently use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies and embed no external fonts or social-media widgets. The website currently creates no account session cookies.

Invitations and device access

Access currently uses single-use invitations and an upload token per device access. We store a device ID, creation time, revocation status and token hash, plus the invitation hash, expiry and used status. The full token is displayed when issued; keep it private. Short-lived request limits use hashed IP addresses or device IDs. Old limit records are cleaned up on subsequent requests after 24 hours.

These records secure the requested upload function and prevent unauthorized access (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Credentials are needed for the lifetime of access. After revocation, minimal revocation and processing records may remain to prevent replay and restoration; they are not used in public statistics.

Voluntary measurements and publication

In the companion you select which characters to upload and whether their name and realm may be published. Uploads contain persistent character and collection IDs, export sequences, addon version, timestamps, effect IDs, area groups, counts, combat durations and retained combat segments. Name and realm are transmitted only when named publication is selected.

The purpose is long-term community analysis of proc observations. Voluntary uploads and optional publication of names are based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can still use the website and addon without uploading. Aggregated measurements and, if selected, character names and realms are public. Full raw files, WoW account folder names, installation paths and chat are not uploaded.

“Anonymous” in the app means pseudonymous: persistent IDs still link your contributions. Measurements are retained for long-term analysis until you request deletion or that purpose ceases. The server has no rolling 300-fight limit. Publicly downloaded copies cannot be fully recalled.

Upload processing, deletion and backups

Uploads first enter a protected queue and are then processed. Processed raw packets are removed; stored measurements and processing receipts remain for analysis, confirmation and deduplication. Cancelled or failed jobs may remain until cleanup.

The companion lets you hide published names or delete the contributions of your device access and revoke that access. Stopping automatic uploads alone does not delete existing data. If you lack access, contact us by email; we verify ownership before disclosing or deleting data. Withdrawal applies prospectively and does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

The database and pending uploads are backed up for recovery (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). These backups currently have no automatic time-based deletion; deleted data can therefore remain in separate backups. Deletion requests must also be considered when handling backups. Deletions and revocations must be reapplied before a restored service is made available. Backups are not used for public analysis.

Email contact and current sign-in status

For email enquiries we process the sender address, message content and technical delivery data to handle your request (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Messages to gabbaproc@kellerkind.info are delivered to the project mailbox and also forwarded to the operator’s mailbox mail@nilskeller.de. Both run on the self-managed mail server. Correspondence is retained as long as needed to handle the enquiry and, where necessary, resolve claims.

Mail-server logs rotate weekly; four rotated files remain in addition to the current file. A project mailbox exists, but email, Discord, GitHub, Google, Microsoft and Apple sign-in are not active yet. Their data flows and retention periods will be added here before activation.

Companion, downloads and external links

The companion locally stores settings, discovered file paths, upload queues and confirmations. Its upload token is stored in the operating system keyring or, if unavailable, only for the current session. The addon stores measurements locally in WoW SavedVariables. Server-side deletion does not automatically erase local files.

Downloads and update checks use GitHub, which receives technical connection data such as your IP address. CurseForge and other linked websites receive such data when you visit them. Their own privacy notices apply, and processing outside the EU is possible. Simply viewing our page does not send data to these linked providers.

Your rights

Subject to the statutory conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and data portability (Articles 15–20 GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article 21 GDPR). Contact gabbaproc@kellerkind.info.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, particularly where you live, work or suspect an infringement (Article 77 GDPR). The Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information is a contact point for the operator in Hesse.

There is no automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects. Automated plausibility checks may exclude measurements from public statistics; you can contact us for clarification.
