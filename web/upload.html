Upload export

You need to sign in to upload.

Export in WoW, then /reload or log out. Select your character’s SavedVariables/GabbaProcTracker.lua file. It is read locally in your browser; only the previewed, filtered data is sent after you click Upload.

Typical file locations (replace ACCOUNT, REALM and CHARACTER with your folders):

Windows
C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\WTF\Account\ACCOUNT\REALM\CHARACTER\SavedVariables\GabbaProcTracker.lua
macOS
/Applications/World of Warcraft/_classic_era_/WTF/Account/ACCOUNT/REALM/CHARACTER/SavedVariables/GabbaProcTracker.lua
Linux · Faugus
~/Faugus/battlenet/drive_c/Program Files (x86)/World of Warcraft/_classic_era_/WTF/Account/ACCOUNT/REALM/CHARACTER/SavedVariables/GabbaProcTracker.lua

Your installation folder may differ. On Linux, look inside your Wine/Lutris/Bottles prefix. Choose the file under the character folder, not the account-wide SavedVariables folder.

Unchecked: pseudonymous upload. Previously published names are not removed by this choice; use the privacy controls to hide them.
