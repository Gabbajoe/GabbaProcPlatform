About GabbaProc

Count the sparks!

GabbaProc is a private community project for World of Warcraft Classic Era and Hardcore. Players collect proc measurements with the addon and share them through the companion app or website.

Open source

Both projects are open source. You can read the code, report issues or contribute on GitHub.

Website & companion

The website, data processing and desktop app for uploading your measurements.

The WoW addon that records proc measurements and exports them for sharing.
